Make the sky turn

  1. Print and cut both red outlines. Cut the holder’s two windows; retain the narrow supports and center hub.
  2. Place the smaller holder over the star wheel. Loosely fasten the center holes with a paper brad.
  3. Turn a date tick on the outer rim to an hour tick on the holder. Compass letters mark the horizon; the hub and supports hide a small strip.
